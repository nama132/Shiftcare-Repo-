Our care process

Getting started takes about a week.

No long-term contracts, no enrollment fees, and nothing owed for the initial assessment.

📞
STEP 01

Free in-home assessment

A registered nurse spends about 60 minutes in the home reviewing health history, medications, mobility, and safety. No cost, no obligation.

📋
STEP 02

A care plan built for one person

You'll receive a written plan with hours, daily routine, and care goals — then meet the caregiver we've matched before the first visit begins.

🌿
STEP 03

Care begins, with real oversight

Visit notes after every shift, supervisory nurse check-ins every 60 days, and a 24/7 on-call line staffed by our own team — never an answering service.

Services we provide

Support that adapts as needs change

Start with a few hours a week. Scale up to around-the-clock care without ever changing agencies.

🛁

Personal care & hygiene

Dignified, hands-on help with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, and safe transfers. All personal care is supervised by a registered nurse.

☕

Companion care

Conversation, meal preparation, light housekeeping, laundry, errands, and rides to appointments, church, or the barber.

🧠

Alzheimer's & dementia care

Caregivers complete 16 hours of specialized memory-care training. Structured routines, redirection techniques, and wandering-risk planning.

💊

Medication reminders

Timed prompts, refill coordination with your pharmacy, and a written log so nothing gets missed or doubled up.

🏥

Post-hospital recovery

Transitional support after surgery or a hospital stay — discharge instructions followed, mobility encouraged, readmission risks watched closely.

🌙

Respite & 24/7 live-in

Overnight awake care, weekend relief, and live-in arrangements so family caregivers can rest, travel, or simply catch their breath.
